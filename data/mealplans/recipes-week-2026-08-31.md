## 2. Cheesy Chicken and Rice Bake
**~30 minutes with fresh rice, ~20 with rice already cooked · saucepan + one baking dish**

**You need:** 8 oz Trader Joe's Just Chicken, pulled · 1 1/2 cups cooked white rice (1/2 cup dry) · 3 cups broccoli florets · 1/2 cup shredded cheddar · 1/4 cup Trader Joe's Garlic Spread & Dip · 1/4 cup chicken broth · salt, pepper

1. **Start the rice** (skip if you have leftovers). Rinse 1/2 cup long-grain white rice, add it to a saucepan with 1 cup water and a pinch of salt, bring to a boil, cover, and drop to the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Pull it off and let it sit covered 5 more. Don't lift the lid before then.
2. **Heat the oven to 425°F** while the rice cooks.
3. **Prep while you wait.** Cut the broccoli into bite-size florets. Pull the Just Chicken apart into shreds with your fingers or two forks.
4. **Par-cook the broccoli** 3 minutes — steamer basket, or a covered pan with a splash of water. You want it bright green and still firm; it finishes in the oven. **Drain it well and shake it dry.** Wet broccoli is the difference between a bake and a soup.
5. **Mix everything** in an 8x8 baking dish (or an oven-safe skillet): rice, chicken, broccoli, the Garlic Spread & Dip, the chicken broth, half the cheddar, and a good pinch of salt and pepper. Stir until the spread is evenly distributed and the rice looks coated, not dry.
6. **Top with the rest of the cheddar** and bake 12–15 minutes, until it's bubbling at the edges.
7. **Broil 2 minutes** for browned spots on top. Stay at the oven door — it goes from golden to burnt fast.
8. **Rest 5 minutes** before serving. It sets up as it cools.

**Notes:** The Garlic Spread & Dip is standing in for canned cream-of-something soup — it's much thicker, so the splash of broth in step 5 is what turns it into a sauce. Don't skip it. To keep this inside the weeknight window, use leftover rice or a microwave rice pouch and it's a 20-minute dinner. Leftovers reheat well with a tablespoon of water stirred in first. If you want a crunchy top, crush a handful of gluten-free crackers or corn chips over the cheese before it bakes.
