# Cooking Instructions — Week of August 17 — August 23

Step-by-step for each dinner in `mealplan-week-2026-08-17.md`.

**All quantities below serve 2** (the meal plan lists per-serving amounts; these are doubled).
**Everything here is gluten-free** — the only items to check labels on are the BBQ sauce, marinara, and taco seasoning.
**Assumed pantry staples:** olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic, onion powder, cumin, curry powder, ground ginger.

---

## 1. Beef Tacos
**~20 minutes · one skillet**

**You need:** 9 oz ground beef · 6 corn tortillas · 6 tbsp shredded cheddar · 3 cups shredded romaine · 1 cup cherry tomatoes, diced · 2 tbsp Trader Joe's Taco Seasoning Mix · 1/4 cup water

1. Shred the romaine and dice the tomatoes. Set them on the table — tacos come together fast.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Add the ground beef and break it into small pieces with a wooden spoon. Cook 6–8 minutes until browned with no pink left.
3. Tip out any excess fat, then sprinkle the taco seasoning over the beef. Add 1/4 cup water, stir, and simmer 2–3 minutes until the liquid thickens into a sauce that clings to the meat. Taste and adjust salt.
4. Warm the tortillas: set them directly on a gas burner for ~20 seconds per side until lightly charred, or stack them in a damp paper towel and microwave 30 seconds.
5. Build each taco with beef, cheddar, romaine, and tomatoes.

**Notes:** Corn tortillas tear when cold — warming isn't optional. Leftover beef keeps 3 days and is excellent over rice.

## 2. BBQ Chicken with Potato Wedges & Green Beans
**~40 minutes · oven + one skillet**

**You need:** 10 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs · 1 lb russet potatoes · 3 cups green beans, trimmed · 4 tbsp Trader Joe's Kansas City Style BBQ Sauce · olive oil, salt, pepper

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Scrub the potatoes and cut each into 8 wedges.
2. Toss the wedges with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread them on a sheet pan in a single layer, cut-side down, leaving space between them. Roast 20 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, pat the chicken thighs dry and season both sides with salt and pepper.
4. At the 20-minute mark, flip the wedges and push them to one side of the pan. Add the green beans, tossed with a little oil and salt, to the other side. Return to the oven for 15 more minutes.
5. While that finishes, heat a skillet over medium-high with a teaspoon of oil. Sear the thighs 5–6 minutes per side until cooked through (165°F). In the last minute, brush both sides with the BBQ sauce and let it caramelize — don't add it earlier or the sugar burns.
6. Rest the chicken 5 minutes, slice, and serve with the wedges and beans.

**Notes:** Cut-side down on the sheet pan is what makes the wedges crisp. If your green beans are thin, add them at 25 minutes instead of 20.

## 3. Chicken & Broccoli Stir-Fry with Rice
**~20 minutes · one skillet**

**You need:** 12 oz chicken breast, thinly sliced · 2 cups Trader Joe's Frozen Jasmine Rice · 3 cups broccoli florets · 1 cup sliced carrots · 3 tbsp Trader Joe's Gluten Free Tamari · 1 tsp ground ginger (or fresh, grated) · 2 cloves garlic, minced · 1/4 cup water

1. Slice the chicken thinly against the grain. Mince the garlic, slice the carrots, cut broccoli into bite-size florets.
2. Microwave the frozen jasmine rice per the package (about 3 minutes) and set aside covered.
3. Heat a large skillet or wok over high with 1 tbsp oil. Add the chicken in a single layer and let it sit undisturbed 2 minutes to brown, then stir-fry 2–3 more minutes until just cooked. Move it to a plate.
4. Add another teaspoon of oil, then the broccoli and carrots. Stir-fry 2 minutes, then add 1/4 cup water and cover for 2–3 minutes to steam the broccoli bright green and crisp-tender.
5. Uncover, add the garlic and ginger, and stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
6. Return the chicken to the pan, pour in the tamari, and toss 1 minute to coat everything. Serve over the rice.

**Notes:** High heat and not crowding the pan is the whole trick. Use tamari, never regular soy sauce — soy sauce contains wheat.

## 4. Spaghetti with Chicken Sausage & Marinara
**~25 minutes · pot + skillet**

**You need:** 4 links Trader Joe's Roasted Garlic Chicken Sausage, sliced into coins · 4 oz Trader Joe's Brown Rice Pasta · 1 cup Trader Joe's Roasted Garlic Marinara · 2 cups zucchini, half-moons · 4 tbsp shaved parmesan

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
2. While it heats, slice the sausage into 1/2-inch coins and cut the zucchini into half-moons.
3. Cook the pasta according to the package, but start tasting 2 minutes early — brown rice pasta goes from firm to mushy quickly. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining, and don't rinse.
4. Meanwhile, heat a skillet over medium-high with a teaspoon of oil. Sear the sausage coins 3–4 minutes until browned on both sides (they're pre-cooked, so you're just crisping them).
5. Add the zucchini and cook 3–4 minutes until it softens and picks up a little color.
6. Pour in the marinara, reduce to a simmer, and warm through 2 minutes.
7. Add the drained pasta to the skillet and toss, splashing in pasta water a tablespoon at a time until the sauce coats every strand. Top with parmesan.

**Notes:** GF pasta releases more starch — that reserved pasta water is what turns the sauce silky instead of watery. Cook it last-minute; it doesn't hold well.

## 5. Chicken Fajitas
**~25 minutes · one skillet**

**You need:** 10 oz chicken breast, sliced into strips · 1 1/2 cups Trader Joe's Cilantro Lime Rice · 3 cups bell peppers, sliced (mixed colors) · 2 cups yellow onion, sliced · 1/2 avocado · 6 tbsp Trader Joe's Salsa Autentica · 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp chili powder · 1 lime

1. Slice the chicken into 1/2-inch strips. Toss with the cumin, chili powder, 1/2 tsp salt, and a squeeze of lime. Let it sit while you cut the vegetables.
2. Slice the peppers and onions into strips roughly the same size as the chicken.
3. Heat a large skillet over high with 1 tbsp oil until it shimmers. Add the peppers and onions in one layer and cook 5–6 minutes, stirring only occasionally — you want real char marks, not steamed vegetables. Move them to a plate.
4. Add another teaspoon of oil, then the chicken in a single layer. Cook 3 minutes without moving it, then stir and cook 2–3 minutes more until done.
5. Return the vegetables to the pan, toss together for 1 minute, and finish with another squeeze of lime.
6. Heat the cilantro lime rice per the package. Serve the fajita mix over the rice with salsa and sliced avocado.

**Notes:** The char is the flavor — resist stirring constantly. Get the pan hot before anything goes in.

## 6. Coconut Curry Chicken over Rice
**~30 minutes · one deep skillet + rice pot**

**You need:** 10 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks · 1 1/2 cups cooked basmati rice · 2 cups bell peppers, sliced · 4 cups baby spinach · 2/3 cup Trader Joe's Organic Coconut Milk · 2 tbsp curry powder · 2 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tsp ground ginger · 1/2 cup chicken broth

1. Start the basmati rice (or microwave a frozen pouch at the end).
2. Cut the chicken thighs into 1-inch chunks and season with salt and pepper.
3. Heat a deep skillet over medium-high with 1 tbsp oil. Brown the chicken 5–6 minutes, turning once or twice. It doesn't need to be cooked through yet.
4. Add the peppers and cook 3 minutes until they soften slightly.
5. Push everything to one side, add the garlic, ginger, and curry powder to the empty space, and stir 30 seconds in the oil until fragrant — this blooms the spices and is the difference between a good and a dull curry.
6. Pour in the coconut milk and broth, stir everything together, and simmer 8–10 minutes until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon and the chicken is cooked through.
7. Stir in the spinach a handful at a time until wilted, about 1 minute. Taste for salt. Serve over rice.

**Notes:** Shake the coconut milk can before opening. If the sauce tightens too much, loosen it with a splash of broth.

## 7. Loaded Sweet Potatoes with Black Beans & Cheddar
**~50 minutes mostly hands-off · oven (or 15 min with the microwave shortcut)**

**You need:** 2 large sweet potatoes · 1 1/2 cups Trader Joe's Organic Black Beans, drained and rinsed · 1/2 cup shredded cheddar · 1 cup frozen sweet corn · 6 tbsp Trader Joe's Organic Taco Sauce · 1 tsp cumin

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Scrub the sweet potatoes, prick each several times with a fork, and set them directly on the oven rack with a sheet pan underneath to catch drips. Bake 45–50 minutes until a knife slides in with no resistance.
   - **Shortcut:** microwave them 8–10 minutes, turning halfway, then finish 10 minutes in the oven for a better skin.
2. About 10 minutes before the potatoes are done, warm the beans in a small saucepan with the cumin and a splash of water, 5 minutes. Mash a few against the side of the pot to thicken them.
3. Heat the corn in a dry skillet over high heat, 3–4 minutes, until some kernels blister and brown.
4. Split each potato lengthwise, push the ends toward each other to open it up, and fluff the flesh with a fork.
5. Load with beans, corn, and cheddar — the heat of the potato melts the cheese. Finish with taco sauce.

**Notes:** Bake the potatoes on the rack, not wrapped in foil — foil steams them soft instead of concentrating the flavor. This one reheats better than anything else on the list.
